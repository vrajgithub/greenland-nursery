# Greenland Nursery and Horticulture Services Hyderabad

Official website for **Greenland Nursery and Horticulture Services**, a premier botanical boutique and horticulture consultancy located in Hyderabad. Established in 1985, we have been greening homes and commercial spaces across Telangana for 39 years.

**Live Website:** [https://vrajgithub.github.io/greenland-nursery/](https://vrajgithub.github.io/greenland-nursery/)
**Official Domain:** [greenlandnurseryofficial.com](http://greenlandnurseryofficial.com)

---

## 🌿 Business Heritage
Since **1985**, Greenland Nursery has been a trusted name in SR Nagar, Hyderabad. We specialize in acclimatized indoor plants, exotic outdoor trees, and professional horticulture services. Our legacy is built on quality, expertise, and a deep love for nature.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Modern Responsive Design:** Built with Tailwind CSS for a premium, mobile-first experience.
- **Interactive Plant Finder:** A custom-coded "Plant Match" quiz to help customers find the right plant for their specific sunlight and care needs.
- **Dynamic Catalog:** A searchable and filterable plant gallery powered by a centralized JavaScript database.
- **Top 10 Sellers:** A dedicated ranking page showcasing Hyderabad's most popular plants.
- **Horticulture Services:** Detailed breakdown of professional services including Landscape Design and Plant Doctor consultations.
- **Localized Pricing:** Consistent "Starting from ₹XXX/-" format tailored for the Indian market.
- **Heritage Branding:** Custom UI elements celebrating our 39-year history (Est. 1985).
